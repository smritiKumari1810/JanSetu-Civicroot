## 📜 Word-for-Word Presentation Script

### 1. The Hook & The Problem (0:00 - 0:45)
> *"Respected judges, imagine a water pipe breaks underneath a busy street.  
> Over 48 hours, 40 different citizens file complaints: one complains about low pressure, another about muddy tap water, and another about a flooded sidewalk.  
> 
> In today's municipal systems, those are treated as 40 separate, disconnected tickets. Three different repair vans are dispatched on three different days to patch symptoms, while the main pipeline continues to collapse.  
> 
> We are **Team Mindshift**, and we built **JanSetu + CivicRoot AI**—transforming citizen complaints into preventive civic intelligence."*

---

### 2. The Solution & Live Demo (0:45 - 1:45)
*(Action: Open screen to `/report`)*

> *"Our platform operates in two synchronized layers:  
> 
> **Layer 1 is JanSetu — The Citizen Portal.**  
> Citizens don't need to navigate complex government departments. They select a category, drop a pin using our **'📍 Auto-detect GPS'**, snap a live photo, or simply record a **🎙️ multi-lingual voice note**.  
> *(Click 'Submit')*  
> Evidence is instantly streamed to **Cloudinary CDN**, and citizens receive an immediate tracking ID like `#JS-8821` to monitor progress on their **'My Reports'** page with transparent status chips.  
> 
> **Layer 2 is CivicRoot AI — The Municipal Command Center.**  
> *(Click 'CivicRoot AI' in top navigation to open `/gov-dashboard`)*  
> This is where the municipal intelligence comes alive.  
> 
> In seconds, CivicRoot's AI engine analyzes disparate reports across the city. Instead of 40 raw tickets, it detects **localized failure clusters**, rates their **risk severity**, and gives city engineers the exact root-cause diagnosis and recommended preventive action on our **Live Civic Risk Map**."*

---

### 3. Architecture & Tech Innovation (1:45 - 2:30)
*(Action: Show the Risk Matrix table on the dashboard)*

> *"Behind the scenes:  
> - We built a responsive, mobile-first **React 19 + Node.js Express REST architecture** with full **React Error Boundary** protection for zero-crash reliability.  
> - Our data is persisted on **MongoDB Atlas**, and evidence is optimized globally on **Cloudinary CDN**.  
> - Our AI pipeline uses a **Triple-Tier Multi-Cloud Resilience Engine**: **Google Gemini 3.6 Flash** as primary, **Groq Cloud (Llama 3.3 70B)** as high-speed secondary fallback, and our **built-in local clustering algorithm** for 100% offline availability.  
> - We have an automated test suite with **100% test coverage** across 9 test cases and dedicated `/health` monitoring."*
